About the QR Code Generator

A QR code is just a way of encoding text as a scannable pattern. Type in a link, a message, or contact info, and this tool draws the matching code and lets you download it as a PNG to print or share.

Frequently asked questions

What can I put into a QR code?

Any text — a plain URL, a phone number, or a short message. Scanning it returns that text to the scanning device.

Is the QR code generated on a server?

No, it's generated locally in your browser and never sent anywhere.

Do QR codes expire?

No — the code encodes your text directly and doesn't depend on this site staying online.
