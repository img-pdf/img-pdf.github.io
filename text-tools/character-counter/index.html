About the Character Counter

Different limits apply in different places — a tweet, a text message, a search-engine meta description. This tool counts as you type and shows how close you are to a few common caps, so you're not guessing or copy-pasting into another app to check.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the byte count differ from the character count?

Characters outside basic ASCII, like emoji or accented letters, can take more than one byte in UTF-8, so byte count is sometimes higher than character count.

Is my text sent anywhere?

No — counting happens locally in your browser as you type.
