About the Word Counter

Useful for hitting an essay's word limit, checking a social post fits a character cap, or estimating how long an article takes to read. Everything updates live as you type, without a "count" button to click.

Frequently asked questions

How is reading time calculated?

Using an average reading speed of 200 words per minute, a commonly used estimate for adult silent reading.

Is my text stored or sent anywhere?

No — the count happens locally in your browser as you type. Nothing is saved or transmitted.
