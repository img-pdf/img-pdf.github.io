How to Convert PDF to JPG or PNG Online in 3 Steps

  1. Open your PDF document: Click the upload box or drag your PDF directly into the tool window. The tool reads and parses pages locally in your browser memory.
  2. Select output format and rendering scale: Choose JPG for photos and documents destined for upload forms, or PNG for crisp text, schematics, and diagrams. Pick 1.5x (~150 DPI) for balanced legibility, or 2.0x (~300 DPI) for ultra-sharp typography.
  3. Download single images or complete ZIP: Download any specific page with one click, or leave pages checked and press "Download Selected as ZIP" to receive an organized archive of all converted images.

Understanding Rendering Scale & DPI

Standard PDF documents are measured in points (72 points per inch). When converting vector PDF pages into pixel-based raster graphics (JPG/PNG), the scale factor dictates the output pixel resolution:

  • Standard (1.0x Scale · ~96 DPI): Generates an image matching standard computer display resolution (approx. 800 × 1100 pixels for an A4 page). Ideal for quick previewing and low-bandwidth web sharing.
  • Medium (1.5x Scale · ~150 DPI): Scales dimensions by 150% (approx. 1240 × 1750 pixels). Provides crisp text rendering on high-DPI smartphone and laptop screens while keeping file sizes reasonable.
  • High Resolution (2.0x Scale · ~300 DPI): Doubles pixel dimensions (approx. 1650 × 2330 pixels). Ideal when you need to extract fine print, barcodes, complex legal disclaimers, or print the converted image onto paper.

Technical Specifications, Limits & Best Practices

  • Supported Document Types: Standard PDF files (PDF 1.4 through PDF 2.0 specifications).
  • Memory Guidelines: Processing happens within your browser's private JavaScript heap memory. Documents containing up to 50–100 pages convert swiftly on modern hardware. For giant multi-hundred page books or legal archives, we recommend splitting the PDF into smaller batches first using our Split PDF Tool.
  • Encrypted PDFs: Password-protected or encrypted PDFs cannot be converted until the security password is removed.
  • Scanned Document Legibility: If the original PDF contains blurry or low-resolution scans, increasing the rendering scale to 2.0x will preserve the scanned pixels faithfully, but will not reconstruct missing optical details.
