Why Combine Multiple Images Into a Single PDF?

Loose images (JPG, PNG) can be difficult to manage, easily get separated in email threads, and often print unpredictably depending on viewer software. Packing receipts, assignments, photo galleries, or multi-page paper documents into a standardized PDF ensures universal document presentation across Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, and Android.

Choosing the Best Page Layout

  • Fit to Image: Ideal for digital portfolios, artworks, and screenshots. Each PDF page matches the exact pixel aspect ratio of the underlying graphic with zero white borders.
  • A4 (Standard International): The global standard for resumes, contracts, and assignments across Europe, Asia, Australia, and Africa.
  • US Letter: The standard format for North American corporate and legal filing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert multiple images into a single combined PDF?

Yes. Add as many images as you need, arrange them using the up/down arrows, and ImgPDF concatenates them sequentially into one downloadable PDF document.

What page size settings are available?

You can select "Fit to image" (which sizes each page to the image's exact dimensions), standard "A4" (210 x 297 mm), or US "Letter" (8.5 x 11 in), with customizable margins and auto-orientation.

Are my scanned receipts and photos private?

Yes. Because ImgPDF uses client-side JavaScript (pdf-lib) executing entirely within your web browser, files are never transmitted to a cloud server or stored remotely.
