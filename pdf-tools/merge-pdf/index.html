About Merge PDF

Splitting a document into files by section, or scanning pages separately, often leaves you with several PDFs that really belong together. This tool stitches them into one file, in whatever order you set, without a round trip to a server.

How to merge PDFs

  1. Drag in two or more PDF files, or choose them from your files.
  2. Use the up/down arrows to put them in the right order.
  3. Click Merge PDFs and download the combined file.

Frequently asked questions

How many PDFs can I merge at once?

As many as you add — there's no fixed limit, though very large files take longer since everything processes in your browser.

Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

No. The merge happens entirely in your browser. Your files never leave your device.

Can I change the order of the PDFs?

Yes — use the up/down arrows to reorder files before merging. Pages combine in that order.

Does merging work on password-protected PDFs?

Encrypted PDFs may fail to merge or lose their original restrictions. Remove password protection first if you run into issues.
