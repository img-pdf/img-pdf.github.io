About Merge PDF

Splitting a document into files by section, or scanning pages separately, often leaves you with several PDFs that really belong together. This tool stitches them into one file, in whatever order you set, without a round trip to a server.

How to Merge PDF Files Online

  1. Select PDFs: Drag and drop 2 or more PDF documents into the dropzone above, or click to browse files on your device.
  2. Arrange Sequence: Use the up and down arrow buttons next to each file name to place your documents in the exact desired page order.
  3. Merge Documents: Click Merge PDFs to concatenate all pages locally using the in-browser pdf-lib engine.
  4. Download: Click Download Merged PDF to save your merged document immediately.

Limitations & Troubleshooting

Why did my password-protected PDF fail to merge?

Encrypted PDFs protect the internal document cross-reference table with cryptography. Client-side libraries cannot access or re-serialize encrypted page streams without decryption credentials. Please decrypt or print-to-PDF the protected file before merging.

What is the maximum file size or page count for merging?

Because merging runs inside your browser tab's JavaScript memory sandbox, available device RAM determines performance. Desktop browsers typically merge documents with hundreds of pages effortlessly. On mobile phones, merging very heavy documents (>50MB each) may cause the browser tab to reload due to memory constraints.

Are form fields or digital signatures preserved?

Interactive AcroForms and cryptographic digital signatures are tied to specific byte offsets in the original document. When merging multiple files, pdf-lib compiles pages into a standard PDF stream; dynamic form fields may be flattened to static content.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many PDFs can I merge at once?

As many as you add — there's no fixed limit, though very large files take longer since everything processes in your browser.

Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

No. The merge happens entirely in your browser. Your files never leave your device.

Can I change the order of the PDFs?

Yes — use the up/down arrows to reorder files before merging. Pages combine in that order.

Does merging work on password-protected PDFs?

Encrypted PDFs may fail to merge or lose their original restrictions. Remove password protection first if you run into issues.
