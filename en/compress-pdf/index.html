PDF Compression FAQs

Will compressing my PDF make the text blurry?

No. Vector text elements, TrueType fonts, and line artwork are preserved mathematically. Only heavy embedded raster photos and scanner artifacts are efficiently optimized.

Can I compress password-protected PDF files?

If your PDF is encrypted with an owner or user password, unlock it before compression so our browser-based engine can parse the content streams.
