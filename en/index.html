Frequently Asked Questions

Are my sensitive documents and photos uploaded anywhere?

No. ImgPDF runs 100% on your device using HTML5 Canvas, Web Cryptography, and local WebAssembly libraries. Your files never touch external servers or cloud storage.

Is there a limit on how many images or PDFs I can process?

There are no subscription tiers, payment gates, or artificial daily quotas. Because computation is performed locally in your browser memory, practical limits depend on your device available RAM and processor.

Does PDF or image compression reduce visual sharpness?

ImgPDF uses quantization thresholds that discard imperceptible high-frequency visual data while preserving edge clarity. Typical 75-80% settings reduce file sizes by 60% to 80% with crisp visual fidelity.

How does ImgPDF support its hosting costs if it is completely free?

Because processing runs locally on your client hardware, our server infrastructure costs are minimal—merely hosting lightweight static HTML, CSS, and JS files. Non-intrusive, privacy-respecting advertising may help offset domain and CDN expenses without tracking your identity.
