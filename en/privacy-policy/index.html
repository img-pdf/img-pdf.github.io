Privacy Policy

Your privacy is not just a policy at ImgPDF—it is our core software architecture. We process your documents locally in your browser memory and never upload files to our servers.

Last updated: November 2026

1. In-Browser File Processing Guarantee

When you use any ImgPDF tool (such as our PDF Compressor, Image Resizer, or Converters), your files are loaded directly into your web browser session. No file content, image binary, or PDF stream is ever transmitted to, stored on, or inspected by our servers.

2. Analytics & Performance Measurement

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and Google Tag Manager (GTM) solely to monitor aggregate website traffic, browser compatibility, and general tool usage. We implement strict client-side data filters that prevent any filename, file content, or personal identifier from being sent to analytics providers.

3. Third-Party Services & Hosting

Our website is statically hosted via GitHub Pages and content delivery networks. These services may log standard network requests (such as your IP address and user-agent) necessary to serve static HTML, CSS, and JS assets securely.

4. Contact & Inquiries

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or our client-side architecture, contact us at privacy@img-pdf.org.
