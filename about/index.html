About ImgPDF

ImgPDF is a free, privacy-first suite of digital tools for images, PDF documents, developer utilities, and everyday text operations. Built with modern web standards, every tool on ImgPDF executes directly inside your browser tab without requiring account registration, credit cards, or server uploads.

The Problem with Traditional File Converters

Most commercial file-conversion and compression websites operate on a centralized client-server model: when you compress a photo or merge two PDF contracts, your files are transmitted across the public internet to a remote server farm, processed in a remote queue, and returned via a download link.

This traditional model presents three fundamental problems:

  1. Privacy Risk: Confidential tax returns, bank statements, medical records, and family photos leave your device and sit on third-party servers.
  2. Slow Latency: Uploading and downloading multi-megabyte files across cellular or home networks wastes bandwidth and introduces artificial queue delays.
  3. Paywalls & Artificial Limits: Centralized servers consume expensive compute and storage resources, forcing websites to impose daily usage caps, file-size paywalls, and aggressive subscription traps.

The ImgPDF Architecture: Zero-Server Processing

Modern web browsers—including Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge—now feature high-performance graphics pipelines, WebAssembly execution, and native cryptography engines that rival native desktop utilities. ImgPDF harnesses these standard browser capabilities:

  • HTML5 Canvas 2D Engine: Performs GPU-accelerated bicubic resampling, cropping, and format encoding for JPEG, PNG, and WebP assets directly in memory.
  • Web Crypto API: Generates cryptographically secure entropy for randomized password creation and calculates SHA-1, SHA-256, and SHA-512 cryptographic digests at hardware speeds.
  • Client-Side PDF Assembly & Rendering (pdf-lib & Mozilla PDF.js): Parses, merges, scales, converts, and splits PDF document object streams entirely in local RAM.

Our Privacy Commitment

Because computation occurs on your local device CPU, ImgPDF does not incur per-file compute costs. This allows us to keep the platform free, fast, and unbounded—without ever logging, storing, or inspecting your personal documents.

Who Operates ImgPDF?

ImgPDF is an independent productivity project engineered and maintained by software craftsmen who believe essential everyday file utilities should be accessible to everyone worldwide, regardless of device or budget.
