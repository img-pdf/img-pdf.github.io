About the Image Cropper

Sometimes you only need part of a photo — a profile picture squared off, a screenshot trimmed to the relevant part. Drag the selection box to the area you want, resize it from any corner, and crop.

How to crop an image

  1. Drag your image into the box above, or choose it from your files.
  2. Drag the selection box to move it, or drag a corner handle to resize it.
  3. Pick a fixed ratio if you need one, or leave it on Free.
  4. Click Crop Image and download the result.

Frequently asked questions

Can I crop to a specific ratio like 1:1 for a profile photo?

Yes — choose 1:1, 4:3 or 16:9, or leave it on Free to drag any shape.

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No — cropping happens using your browser's canvas engine. The file stays on your device.

Does cropping reduce image quality?

No — the cropped region is copied at full resolution, not resampled.
