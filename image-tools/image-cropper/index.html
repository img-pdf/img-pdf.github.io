About the Image Cropper

Sometimes you only need part of a photo — a profile picture squared off to 1:1, a screenshot trimmed to the relevant window, or a landscape image adjusted to 16:9. Drag the selection box to the area you want, resize it from any corner, and crop with pixel precision.

How to Crop an Image Online

  1. Select Image: Drag your JPG, PNG, WebP, or GIF image into the dropzone above, or choose it from your files.
  2. Adjust the Crop Box: Drag the selection box to move it, or drag any corner handle to resize the region.
  3. Set Aspect Ratio: Pick a fixed ratio (1:1 for profile pictures, 4:3 for standard cards, 16:9 for widescreen headers) or leave it on Free to drag any custom proportion.
  4. Crop & Download: Click Crop Image and download your perfectly trimmed image.

Limitations & Troubleshooting

Does cropping lower image resolution?

No. ImgPDF maps the bounding crop box directly back to the original source coordinates on a 1:1 pixel scale. Only the unselected surrounding area is trimmed; the selected pixels remain uncompressed and unblurred.

Can I crop animated GIF files?

In-browser canvas processing extracts and crops the first frame of an animated GIF. Multi-frame animated cropping requires dedicated video editing software.

Are mobile touch gestures supported?

Yes. The crop box handles support touch events on iOS and Android devices as well as mouse drag on desktop.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I crop to a specific ratio like 1:1 for a profile photo?

Yes — choose 1:1, 4:3 or 16:9 from the ratio options, or leave it on Free to drag any shape you want.

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No, cropping happens using your browser's canvas engine. The file stays on your device.

Does cropping reduce image quality?

No, the cropped region is copied at full resolution — you're just keeping part of the original pixels, not resampling them.
