Digital Image Resizing Best Practices

Scaling image dimensions is often the fastest way to shrink hefty camera photos down to web-friendly sizes. Modern smartphone cameras capture images at 4000x3000 pixels or higher, but most website containers and email viewports rarely require more than 1200 to 1920 pixels in width.

Why Keeping the Aspect Ratio Locked is Critical

Aspect ratio is the proportional relationship between an image's width and height (such as 16:9, 4:3, or 1:1). Keeping Lock aspect ratio enabled ensures that as you edit width or height, the other dimension automatically recalculates, avoiding awkward stretching or squishing.
